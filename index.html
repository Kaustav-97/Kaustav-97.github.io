01 / About

Compliance that actually holds up in an audit.

I'm an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Lead Auditor with 5+ years of experience in banking (BFSI) and fintech. Most of my work happens where business operations meet the central GRC function: testing IT general controls, running user access reviews, walking external auditors through our controls, and following deficiencies until they're actually fixed.

Today, at OpenLM, I'm leading the company's SOC 2 Type II audit and its ISO 27001 audit, and I design and maintain the ISMS behind them, along with its GDPR compliance. My engineering degree and MBA help me explain technical risk in terms the business can act on.

Bank-wide IT Policy

Managed the bank's IT Policy through its annual review cycle. Revised the bank's IT-owned Data Quality Policy and took it through Board approval. It defines how banking data is classified, what "good quality" means, and the controls that protect data through migration and regulatory reporting.

Data classification Quality dimensions Data quality checks Migration controls Reporting lifecycle
